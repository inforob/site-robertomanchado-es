Claude Code is not just another autocomplete: it's an agent that reads your repository, runs commands and edits files. This book teaches you to work with it the way you'd work with a teammate — giving it context, boundaries and a way to verify what it does.

Written by a backend developer with over fifteen years in production, it goes straight to what actually changes your output: how to structure CLAUDE.md, when to delegate to subagents, how to review the code it generates, and which tasks are not worth automating yet.

What you'll learn

  • Installation, permissions and your first project
  • Writing a CLAUDE.md the agent actually follows
  • Real work prompts vs. toy prompts
  • Long sessions: context, plans and checkpoints
  • Hooks, custom commands and skills
  • Large refactors on legacy code
  • Tests, review and quality control
  • Git, branches and assisted pull requests
  • Integrating MCP and external tools
  • Common mistakes and how to get out of them

Who it's for

For developers with real projects on their hands —backend, frontend or full-stack— who have already tried AI and want to move from one-off tricks to a sustained method. No prior experience with agents required.

Posting an item on Vinted is easy; selling it isn't. The difference comes down to specifics: the photo, the headline, the starting price, the time you post, and how you respond to a haggler.

This guide lays out that method step by step, with real examples of listings that work and ones that don't. No get-rich-quick promises: a repeatable system so your items get seen, get sold, and reach the buyer in good shape.

What you'll learn

  • Preparing and picking what actually sells
  • Photography with natural light, no studio needed
  • Titles and descriptions that surface in search
  • Pricing: margin, shipping and fees
  • When to post and how often to renew
  • Negotiating offers without giving the item away
  • Shipping, packaging and timing
  • Reviews, returns and disputes
  • Spotting problem buyers
  • Scaling to regular selling without burning out

Who it's for

For anyone starting out who wants to get it right from the first listing, and for sellers who are already active but have hit a plateau and can't figure out why their items stopped getting views.
